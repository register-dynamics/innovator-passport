### Supplier needs

"Participants broadly supported the idea of a central assurance service, but its value depended on it replacing existing trust-by-trust processes rather than becoming an additional route. The strongest shared need was to provide standard assurance information once, keep it current and allow different trusts to use it. Participants also stressed that the service must distinguish reusable evidence from trust-specific questions, protect sensitive information and establish consistent expectations for what constitutes acceptable assurance evidence." -- from [Supplier prototype testing report_16_10_26] (https://healthsharedservice.sharepoint.com/:w:/r/sites/GOVCompassAlpha75114/_layouts/15/doc2.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B564E124C-99A0-4AE6-B0D3-4B60328D9FCB%7D&file=Supplier%20prototype%20testing%20report_16_10_26.docx&action=default&mobileredirect=true)

### Trust needs

"The concept was positively received: P1 would ‘absolutely’ use a clear assurance table; P2 called the concept ‘definitely a goer’; P4 strongly supported direct access to standard documents and clear task assignment; P5 said the work could have ‘so much added benefit’; and P10 believed it would enhance and simplify their work. This support was conditional: the service needs to provide one clear and trusted view, standardise common evidence, prevent duplicate requests, integrate with procurement and framework processes, protect sensitive information, and make ownership, status, version and audit history unambiguous. Without these conditions, participants expected it to shift existing work into another system rather than reduce the burden on trusts or suppliers." -- From [Trust prototype testing report_16_10_26](https://healthsharedservice.sharepoint.com/:w:/r/sites/GOVCompassAlpha75114/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B25B99070-8E70-4447-BFA7-27C9D91FC30E%7D&file=Trust%20prototype%20testing%20report_16_10_26.docx&action=default&mobileredirect=true&wdwpf=doclib-c)
