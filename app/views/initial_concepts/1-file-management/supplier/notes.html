Design choices and discussion points

User details

  • This is their first time registering this specific device.
  • Device has already begun passport process (Whatever that may be).
  • For now, we assume they want a place to upload files.

Assumptions

  • Multiple users have access to the file hub.
  • Passporting means something here. There is a notion of a completed passport.
  • Company details are a placeholder.
  • Any user can access all parts of the service (for now).

Discussion points

  • Who has access? How many people have access? What levels of access?
  • We will almost certainly need some sort of login system, right?
  • How do we control/configure what trusts/customers see?
  • Do we envision some sort of dashboard or hub? Would this be useful?
  • What sort of information might we expect to see here? How is this linked to Compass?
  • What might the other end look like for the trust? Do they get a neat version? Are they going to see every single DTAC ever uploaded?
  • What is this file management system? Doesn't feel like a passport in any sense of the word.
  • What does a user see when they click on a file?
