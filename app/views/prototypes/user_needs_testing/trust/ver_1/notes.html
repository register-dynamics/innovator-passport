Design choices

This prototype aims to help a trust open a dialogue or start a relationship with a medtech supplier. One our primary concerns is how we encourage adoption. It is easy to design a service that has an existing userbase, but what about new services such as the innovator passport? This prototype looks at that first interaction between trust and supplier, with the trust initiating.

As an MVP prototype, it was important to gauge from users whether this service would be better than their existing procurement processes (which is mostly email chains and various shared drives).

Feedback for this journey can be viewed on the mural board.
